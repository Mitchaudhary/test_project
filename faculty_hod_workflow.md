# Faculty & HOD/Dean Portal Documentation

This document details the features, administrative tools, and operational workflows built for **Faculty** and **HOD / Dean** roles in the **Lok Jagruti Kendra University (LJU) ERP & Timetable Portal**.

---

## 1. Faculty Portal & Lecture Attendance Console
- **Live Lecture Launch**: Faculty members can initiate active lecture sessions, generating dynamic 15-second rotating QR codes, 6-digit OTP codes, and manual roll-call check-ins to prevent proxy attendance.
- **Proxy & Substitute Duty Management**: Faculty can assign substitute professors when on academic leave or official duty, keeping attendance logs and lecture delivery uninterrupted.
- **Teaching Schedule & Faculty Radar**: Tracks personal teaching schedules across university cohorts and displays real-time lecture delivery status.

## 2. HOD & Dean (Super Admin) Level Workflows
- **Master Timetable & Conflict Engine**: Comprehensive management of university-wide timetables across all programs (MCA, M.Sc. IT, BCA, B.Tech CSE, BBA, MBA, BA-LLB) with real-time slot overlap detection and conflict warnings.
- **Defaulter & Attendance Audit**: University-wide audit dashboard identifying students falling below the mandatory 75% threshold across departments.
- **Academic Calendar & Leave Approvals**: Faculty leave management module allowing HODs/Deans to review, approve, or reject leave applications and assign substitute instructors.
- **Granular Cohort Switcher**: Full administrative overview to inspect and manage curriculum delivery, 40-lecture lesson plans, and department timetables across all semesters.
