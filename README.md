# Lok Jagruti Kendra University (LJU) Ahmedabad
## University Academic ERP, Timetable & Real-Time Attendance Portal

A production-grade, multi-role university management system built for Lok Jagruti Kendra University (LJU). It integrates real-time attendance tracking, anti-proxy QR/OTP verification, master timetable conflict resolution, 40-lecture granular micro-topic syllabus planning, and role-based portals for Students, Faculty, and HODs/Deans.

---

## Key Modules & Features

1. **Multi-Role Authentication Portal**:
   - Secure login supporting **Students** (stream and semester specific), **Faculty Members**, and **HODs / Deans (Super Admins)**.
2. **Real-Time Attendance & Roll Call Engine**:
   - 15-second rotating anti-proxy QR codes, 6-digit OTP verification, and manual classroom roll-call tools.
3. **Master Timetable & Conflict Engine**:
   - University-wide curriculum architecture supporting multiple departments (IT & Computer Applications, Engineering & Technology, Management, Law) with real-time overlap detection.
4. **Granular 40-Lecture Micro-Topic Syllabus & Lesson Plan**:
   - Module-by-module tracking of lecture delivery, completed topics, and student progress pacing.
5. **Defaulter Audit & 75% Ordinance Calculator**:
   - Automated monitoring of attendance thresholds with safe buffer calculations and recovery requirements.
6. **Faculty Leave & Substitute Management**:
   - Leave requests, approvals, and proxy instructor assignments.
